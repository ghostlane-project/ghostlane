### Linux servers: the ghostlane command

Ghostlane now runs headless on Linux: one `ghostlane` binary for amd64, arm64 and armv7, packaged as deb, rpm, apk and Arch packages plus a tarball, with a signed installer (`install.sh` on the release page). Add your subscription with `ghostlane add`, pick a country with `ghostlane connect DE`, and the service joins that country's olcRTC rooms with failover between carriers, reconnecting after a reboot. Two modes: `--proxy` serves a local SOCKS5/HTTP proxy with no privileges; `--tun` routes the whole machine while SSH and the machine's other services keep answering on its public address. VLESS, Hysteria2 and XHTTP lines are listed but not connected yet; they follow in the next version. The CLI is GPL-3.0-or-later because it links sing-box; see `docs/cli.md`.
