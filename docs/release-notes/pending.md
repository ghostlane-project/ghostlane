### The board is where it was left

On an iPhone the app's screen is built again every time the app is returned to: its host lets go of the whole scene in the background, for the memory a tunnel's extension needs more, and builds another on the way back in. Whatever the screen only remembered went with it. 1.0.459 kept the folded lists; two more things were still the screen's own.

- The transport chosen in the chips above the list is kept with the rest of what the app knows about its lists, on every platform, and is there the next time the app opens. On a phone it was back at "All" on every return.
- The list is where it was scrolled to. It was back at its top.

### A line nobody could read

"Previous network measurements · tap Measure to refresh", the line over the lists when the delays on the cards were measured on another network, had a style and no colour, and was drawn in black on a ground a shade off black, on every platform. It has the colour of the other notes now. The tests that lay every screen out and fail on cut text also read the picture, and fail on a text that cannot be told from what it is written on.
