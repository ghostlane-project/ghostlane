### SaluteJazz, a third olcRTC carrier

The app now understands SaluteJazz lines: when your provider's server list includes them, they appear as `· SJ` servers beside Telemost and WB, and need no account.

Locations saved under the old "Jazz" provider now connect through SaluteJazz, the same service under its current name; the separate "Jazz" entry is gone from the picker.

### Detailed debug logs

Logs has a "Detailed debug logs" switch that records DNS, routing and connection decisions, for a report to your provider or to us. It is off by default, and the export scrubs addresses and credentials.
