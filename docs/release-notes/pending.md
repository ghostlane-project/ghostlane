### The board is where it was left

On an iPhone the app's screen is built again every time the app is returned to: its host lets go of the whole scene in the background, for the memory a tunnel's extension needs more, and builds another on the way back in. Whatever the screen only remembered went with it. 1.0.459 kept the folded lists; two more things were still the screen's own.

- The transport chosen in the chips above the list is kept with the rest of what the app knows about its lists, on every platform, and is there the next time the app opens. On a phone it was back at "All" on every return.
- The list is where it was scrolled to. It was back at its top.

### A line nobody could read

"Previous network measurements · tap Measure to refresh", the line over the lists when the delays on the cards were measured on another network, had a style and no colour, and was drawn in black on a ground a shade off black, on every platform. It has the colour of the other notes now. The tests that lay every screen out and fail on cut text also read the picture, and fail on a text that cannot be told from what it is written on.

### The traffic chart counts the second it draws

The chart draws a second of traffic as the difference between two readings of the tunnel's counters, and it took its readings from a value each platform refreshed on a timer of its own: every three seconds on an iPhone, every fifteen on Android. So on an iPhone one bar in three held three seconds of traffic and the two beside it nothing, with "0 B/s" beside them two seconds out of three; on Android it was one bar in fifteen. The counters are now read by the screen's own second, on both.

- A reading that comes late is the traffic of every second since the last one, in equal shares, and not one bar holding all of it. That is what a phone's chart shows of the time the app spent in the background.
- The chart and the totals are kept for as long as the session lasts. On an iPhone they were the screen's own and the screen is built again on every return to the app: leave with a session up, come back, and the chart was an empty rule.
- The totals go on when the tunnel under a session is built again, and past four gigabytes on an iPhone, where the system counts a tunnel's bytes in thirty-two bits and the totals went back to nothing.
- Where nothing counts a session's traffic — a desktop or an Android phone in proxy mode — the status strip has no chart. Since 1.0.459 it stood open for one that never came: an empty rule and two rates reading "…" for as long as the session lasted.
