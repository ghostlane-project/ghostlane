### Android and desktop: Bypass Iran and Bypass China

Next to Bypass Russia, the routing setting now offers Bypass Iran and Bypass China. Sites and addresses in the chosen country, and your local network, connect directly; everything else goes through the tunnel. The lists ship with the app, so they work offline. On a computer they apply in system proxy mode and in the macOS tunnel; the Windows and Linux tunnels still send everything through the tunnel. The iPhone app is unchanged.

### Android: bypass modes on olcRTC, from one process

With a bypass mode on (Russia, Iran or China), an olcRTC connection no longer starts a second routing process in front of itself: the connection sends the chosen country's sites and your local network straight out by itself, as the iPhone app does. One process less to start and keep alive, and the bypass now also works in proxy mode, where olcRTC used to send everything through the tunnel.

### macOS and Windows: a window and a tray icon that fit the system

On a Mac the menu bar icon is now monochrome, white or black like the icons next to it, and the window's title bar is the app's own dark colour with the traffic lights on it; the tray menu and file dialogs follow dark mode. On Windows the tray icon is white or black to match the taskbar, and the title bar is dark (exactly the app's colour on Windows 11).
